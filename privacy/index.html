Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026. Service area: New York / Tri-State.

Arc Signage Co. LLC (“Arc,” “we,” “us”) operates arcsignco.com. This policy describes what we collect through our forms and tools, how we use it, and how to reach us. This is a plain-language summary for the website; have counsel review it before relying on it for compliance.

Who we are

Arc Signage Co. LLC, a New York limited liability company, at 1974 Crotona Ave A, Bronx, NY 10457. Phone (347) 450-2110. Email arc@arcsignco.com.

What we collect

Depending on the form you use, we may collect:

  • Name, email, and phone number
  • Project address, scope notes, and timing
  • Photos or artwork you upload (facades, vehicles, logos, sign mockups)
  • Selections you make in the Free Sign Preview, Arc Visualizer, awning builder, or winter-enclosure quote tool
  • Optional SMS consent choices on the visualizer lead form

Forms include the homepage quote-request form, awning-quote, enclosure-quote, sign-estimate-request (Free Sign Preview), and visualizer-quote / visualizer-report.

How submissions are stored

Netlify Forms receives and stores submissions from the static forms on this site. Visualizer API leads and generated preview images are also stored in Netlify Blobs for follow-up. We use the information to respond to quotes, prepare estimates, and operate the tools you used.

Analytics

Some pages load GoatCounter for privacy-friendly traffic counts (no Google Analytics ID is configured on the marketing site today). If we enable Google Analytics later, this policy will be updated. The visualizer and server functions do not add separate advertising trackers.

Bot protection

When enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a human is submitting the Arc Visualizer generate and lead requests. Turnstile is operated by Cloudflare under its own terms.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to run the site (for example Netlify hosting, email delivery, and image APIs used by the visualizer) or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep submissions as long as needed for active projects and business records. To request deletion of form data tied to your contact information, email arc@arcsignco.com from the same address you used on the form, or call (347) 450-2110.

Children

This site is for businesses and property owners, not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this page. The effective date at the top will change when we do.

See also our Terms of Use.

Full-service signage for New York / Tri-State. Arc does not file permits or stamp drawings.
