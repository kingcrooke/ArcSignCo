Digital Signage | New York / Tri-State

Digital signage for touch directories, wayfinding, and video walls

Arc plans and delivers networked displays for campuses, hospitals, foundations, and commercial buildings: touch directories and kiosks, digital wayfinding, room and event boards, donor walls and video walls, plus the content system behind them. Arc can customize the content management system, connect calendars and line-of-business data, produce and schedule content, provision media players, and provide remote support after launch. Physical screens and construction are coordinated with your integrator or GC; Arc stays your single point of contact for the digital scope.

FAQ

Digital signage questions

What digital signage does Arc provide?

Arc scopes and delivers touch directories and kiosks, digital wayfinding, room and event boards, donor walls and video walls, a custom or customized content management system, integrations with calendars and line-of-business systems, ongoing content service, media player setup, and remote support. Display hardware and physical installation are coordinated with your AV integrator or GC when the job needs them.

Can Arc sync room boards to Outlook or Google Calendar?

Yes. Room and event boards can pull schedules from common calendar platforms and event-management tools when the project specifies them. Arc confirms the data sources, refresh rules, and on-screen layouts during scoping.

Does Arc sell display hardware?

Arc focuses on software, content, players, and support. Screens, mounts, and construction are specified with your integrator or GC. Arc can recommend player models and coordinate provisioning with the install team.

Who owns the client relationship?

You work with Arc from discovery through launch and support. Proposals, training, and day-to-day contact stay under the Arc Signage Co brand.

What should I send for a digital signage quote?

Send floor plans or a campus map, a list of screens or locations, calendar or data sources, brand guidelines, photos of each wall or kiosk location, your deadline, and the street address. Email jc@arcsignco.com with the project address in the subject line, or call or text (347) 450-2110. Arc's 24-hour quote target starts once Arc has photos with measurements (or drawings, if you have them) and the project street address.
