Digital Signage | New York / Tri-State

Digital signage for touch directories, wayfinding, and video walls

Arc plans digital signage for campuses, hospitals, foundations, and commercial buildings in New York and the tri-state area — touch directories and kiosks, wayfinding screens, room and event boards, donor walls, and video walls. Arc handles scoping, layouts, and coordination with your architect and GC. Your AV integrator installs the displays and related AV work.

Process

From discovery to live screens

  1. 01

    Discover

    Locations, audiences, data sources, brand rules, and success metrics.

  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes, motion rules, and content templates aligned with architects and AV.

  3. 03

    Coordinate

    Scope packages for your AV integrator, GC, and architect; confirm mounting and power locations.

  4. 04

    Content

    On-screen layouts, naming, and brand rules aligned before install.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Walk-through with your team after the AV integrator installs and powers on displays.

FAQ

Digital signage questions

What digital signage does Arc provide?

Arc scopes touch directories and kiosks, digital wayfinding, room and event boards, donor walls, and video walls for campuses, hospitals, foundations, and commercial buildings. Arc scopes and coordinates the project; your AV integrator installs displays and related AV work.

Does Arc sell display hardware?

Arc focuses on scoping, layouts, and coordination. Screens, mounts, and construction are specified with your AV integrator or GC. Arc stays your point of contact for the signage scope.

Who owns the client relationship?

You work with Arc from discovery through launch. Proposals and day-to-day contact stay under the Arc Signage Co brand.

What should I send for a digital signage quote?

Send floor plans or a campus map, a list of screens or locations, brand guidelines, photos of each wall or kiosk location, your deadline, and the street address. Email jc@arcsignco.com with the project address in the subject line, or call or text (347) 450-2110. Arc's 24-hour quote target starts once Arc has photos with measurements (or drawings, if you have them) and the project street address.
