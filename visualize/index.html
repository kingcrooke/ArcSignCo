FAQ

Visualizer questions

What is the Arc Visualizer?

It makes a concept image of a vehicle wrap, a storefront sign, or window graphics from a description or a photo you upload. Your logo file is placed on the image by the page. It is an AI-generated visual reference, not a proof or a quote.

Does the visualizer show a price?

No. It shows Custom quote. Pricing comes in a formal written estimate after a site survey. This preview is a concept only.

What should you send with the preview?

Your name, phone, email, and when you want to talk. Arc follows up from arc@arcsignco.com. For a measured storefront drawing without AI, use the Free Sign Preview, or email jc@arcsignco.com, or call or text (347) 450-2110.
