FAQ

Institutional signage questions

Can Arc work from an architect's schedule?

Yes. Send the schedule, floor plans, and spec section.

Can Arc handle a project built in phases?

Arc can phase shop drawings to construction milestones when dates are provided.

Who owns message content?

The owner supplies the sign text, and Arc makes signs to the approved schedule.
