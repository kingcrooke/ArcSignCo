# Crooke heritage site — content policy (Phase 1)

This folder holds **static markup only**. Do not commit:

- Family photo scans or originals
- Document scans (IDs, certificates, immigration papers)
- DNA raw data or detailed ethnicity reports
- Audio/video interview files, except a cleaned voice note the family asked to publish on that person's own page, with the transcript beside the player

Shareable pages must not display birthdates, phone numbers, email addresses, street addresses, government document numbers, or DNA details for living relatives.

Media for a future vault should be served from private storage (not this public git repository).
