# GWB Instagram feed slide standards

Use for every GWB Fantasy **feed** graphic (not Stories). Match the locked look; do not regress.

## Canvas
1. Size **1080×1350** only (never Story 9:16 letterboxed as a feed post).
2. Side margin about 56px.
3. Measure the full content stack, then center it **horizontally and vertically** on the canvas.

## Type
1. Hero titles: **Anton** (hero words like FRANKIE ZONE around size 72).
2. Section / status labels: **Bebas Neue**.
3. Body: **Inter** (avoid Montserrat Alternates — kerning collisions).
4. Add extra leading beyond `textbbox` (~20%+ of glyph height for Anton/Bebas) so the next line never collides.
5. Prefer middots and plain hyphens; avoid en-dashes and special punctuation that break kerning.

## Hard visual rules
1. Big type first; generous vertical gaps between every element.
2. No overlines, strikethroughs, gold bars through glyphs, or decorative lines that cut text.
3. No text-on-text, no cut-off or clipped words, no stacked-rectangle clutter.
4. Short copy; readable at a glance.
5. Never cram two full team schedules side-by-side if spacing breaks — one team per slide, or a simplified dual column (e.g. losses + next week + collide week only).

## Schedules pattern (when used)
1. Status as **one** line, bigger Bebas: `YOU ARE HERE · 0-2 · UP NEXT` (or equivalent).
2. Closing headline at the **same Anton size** as the hero title; split to two lines if width requires it.

## Publish checklist
1. Caption explains the slide in plain language.
2. @mention every league member who has Instagram; skip anyone without IG.
3. Exactly five hashtags: `#FantasyFootball` `#NFL` `#Football` `#NYC` `#GWBFF`.
4. Visually QA the PNG before showing or posting: no overlaps, no clipped text, stack centered.

## Reference
Keep approved READY PNGs/JPGs and a short STANDARDS note beside the generator for the current campaign so future slides can be compared side-by-side before ship.
