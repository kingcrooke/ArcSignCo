_Label: Process_

## From discovery to live screens

- **01 Discover:** Locations, who uses the screens, where the content comes from, and brand rules.
- **02 Design:** Rough layouts and content templates, checked with the architect and AV integrator.
- **03 Coordinate:** Scope packages for your AV integrator, GC, and architect; confirm mounting and power locations.
- **04 Content:** On-screen layouts, naming, and brand rules aligned before install.
- **05 Launch:** Walk-through with your team after the AV integrator installs and powers on displays.

**Static and digital together:** Arc can coordinate digital walls with [interior wayfinding](/wayfinding-signs/) and [ADA room identification](/ada-signs/) so names, routes, and tactile signs stay consistent.

## Digital signage questions

### What digital signage does Arc provide?

Arc scopes touch directories and kiosks, digital wayfinding, room and event boards, donor walls, and video walls for campuses, hospitals, foundations, and commercial buildings. Arc scopes and coordinates the project; your AV integrator installs displays and related AV work.

### Does Arc sell display hardware?

Arc focuses on scoping, layouts, and coordination. Screens, mounts, and construction are specified with your AV integrator or GC. Arc stays your point of contact for the signage scope.

You work with Arc from the first layout through install.
