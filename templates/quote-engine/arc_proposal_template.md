## Assumptions

- The line items are the scope, including copy, colors, and sizes.
- Where this proposal still shows a range, the price settles after a site survey and confirmed measurements.
- The existing wall or frame can take ordinary anchors. Hidden conditions are handled with you before any extra work.
- Work is scheduled Monday through Friday, between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM.
- You provide site access and someone who can approve the install.
- Permits, stamped drawings, licensed sign hanging, and electrical work, when the job needs them, are allowances performed by licensed partners. Arc coordinates that work. Arc Signage Co does not hold a professional engineering or registered-architecture license, a Licensed Sign Hanger license, or an electrical license.
- An allowance is the budget for that item. Arc will talk with you before going past it.
- Design work is included only when a line item says so.
