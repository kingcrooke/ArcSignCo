## What this range covers

The lines above are the scope. Permits, stamped drawings, licensed sign hanging, and electrical work appear here only as allowances, and only when a line lists them. Licensed partners perform that work. Arc coordinates it.

An allowance is a budget. Arc will talk with you before going past it.

The range uses weekday hours, Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. It leaves out sales tax unless the tax line shows an amount, work that is not listed, and the annual illuminated-sign charge the City may bill to the property owner.
