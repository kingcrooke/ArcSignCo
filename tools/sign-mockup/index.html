      Tips

      Getting a closer estimate

      Shoot straight on

      Stand square to the facade, as far back as you can, with the whole sign area and the thing you measured in the frame. Angled shots make sizes less reliable.

      Measure on the same wall

      Pick something flat and close to the sign location, like the sign area above the storefront, a door, or a window. A long reference gives a better scale than a short one.

      Draw the line end to end

      Zoom in and drag the ends of the scale line exactly onto the edges you measured. The magnifier helps on a phone.

      Treat sizes as a starting point

      The sizes here are estimates from a photo, not field measurements. Fabrication needs verified measurements and shop drawings.

      FAQ

      Free sign preview questions

      What does the free sign preview tool do?

      Upload a storefront photo, set scale from one measurement, choose a sign type or awning shape, and place it on the facade in perspective. Switch between day and night, download a concept PDF, or send a phone approval link. Photos stay on your device unless you create that link.

      Is the mockup a shop drawing or permit check?

      No. The preview is for planning and discussion only. It is not a shop drawing, engineering document, or permit or code review. Final sizes, construction details, and compliance come from measured shop drawings and the project's licensed professionals.

      Who can use the sign mockup tool?

      Store owners, tenants, general contractors, architects, and property teams across New York City's five boroughs and all of New Jersey and Connecticut can use the tool to visualize signage before requesting a formal quote from Arc Signage Co.

      What should you send after previewing a sign?

      Email facade photos with measurements to arc@arcsignco.com with the project street address in the subject line, or use the quote form on arcsignco.com. For questions, call or text (347) 450-2110 or email jc@arcsignco.com.
