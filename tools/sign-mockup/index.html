Upload a photo of the storefront, set the scale from one thing you've measured, pick the kind of sign or awning, and place it on the facade to see its approximate size, built the way it would be fabricated, by day and at night.     Tips

    Getting a closer estimate

    Shoot straight on

    Stand square to the facade, as far back as you can, with the whole sign area and the thing you measured in the frame. Angled shots make sizes less reliable.

    Measure on the same wall

    Pick something flat and close to the sign location, like the sign band, a door, or a window. A long reference gives a better scale than a short one.

    Draw the line end to end

    Zoom in and drag the ends of the scale line exactly onto the edges you measured. The magnifier helps on a phone.

    Treat sizes as a starting point

    The sizes here are estimates from a photo, not field measurements. Fabrication needs verified measurements and shop drawings.
