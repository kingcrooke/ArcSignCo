Services

Full-service signage for NYC projects

From a single storefront sign to a multi-floor ADA package, awning, construction sign, or orientation sticker rollout, Arc handles the scope from planning through punch list.

Exterior Signs

Storefronts, channel letters, blade signs, projecting signs, awnings, cabinets, and dimensional letters.

Interior Wayfinding

Lobby signs, directory boards, directional signs, floor IDs, elevator signage, and room identification.

ADA & Code Signs

ADA room IDs, restroom signs, tactile text, Grade 2 Braille, stairwell IDs, exits, and egress signage.

LED & Illuminated

Front-lit, back-lit, halo-lit, lightbox, cabinet, LED display, and electrical coordination.

Permits & Submittals

DOB permit coordination, shop drawings, and submittal packages, working with licensed sign hangers, engineers, architects, and expediters where the job requires them.

Awnings

Storefront awnings, branded valances, recover layouts, frame coordination, graphics, and install planning.

Construction Signs

Site boards, safety notices, directional signs, hoarding graphics, project IDs, and temporary install scopes.

Orientation Stickers

Door, glass, floor, wall, room, and directional stickers for openings, tenant moves, and punch-list fixes.
